## What to build

**Recovery slice:** R01 · phase R1.

A real provider refresh creates an eligible attributed Edition through protected ingestion, and the operator compares two candidate providers with the existing supply using real reading samples before choosing complementary integrations.

**Demo:** Show the safe baseline paper and direct write-denial checks, then compare matched provider samples and the coverage/permissions scorecard.

## Acceptance criteria

### Protected usable reporting

- [ ] Move shared reporting writes behind trusted ingestion with compatible migrations/configuration; anonymous and ordinary authenticated clients cannot insert, update or delete shared reporting, and runtime privileges remain scoped to that operation.
- [ ] Apply one eligibility policy to newly generated Editions and public previews: reject comment/feed/index pages, generic titles, empty wrappers, malformed dates and insufficient authorized source text; retain publisher/source provenance and bounded rejection reasons.
- [ ] Demonstrate one actual permitted reporting refresh through the normal application path and one attributed new Edition; no fixture-only or HTTP-200-only acceptance. Existing reader-owned data, completed Editions, archive and exports remain accessible under their actual dates.
- [ ] Audit existing supply for future-selection eligibility without silently rewriting completed Editions; segregate test material and report actual provider access, text depth and unresolved output permissions.
- [ ] Through the existing Edition/browser boundary and isolated data, verify trusted-ingestion success, direct public-write denial, two-reader ownership, junk exclusion, source attribution and a retryable unavailable state.
- [ ] Record sanitized commit/date-stamped evidence, migration compatibility and rollback steps; keep secrets out of client assets and preserve the established quota protections.

### Real provider comparison

- [ ] Select two evaluation candidates from GNews, NewsData.io, Currents and Mediastack without purchasing plans; verify account-specific access and record permission for evaluation, public display, customer-facing AI Briefings, PDFs and persistent archives separately.
- [ ] Run matched country/language/topic/time-window cases against both candidates and the existing adapter, accounting for plan delays; report useful unique events, additional publishers, missing-interest/region coverage, usable source text, overlap, errors, requests/credits and cost/capacity implications.
- [ ] Inspect at least 30 live candidate records across the comparison where allowed, and produce attributable readable samples rather than only JSON field checks or raw response counts; report unmet access/sample prerequisites honestly.
- [ ] Distinguish publisher country from evidence-supported event geography and document each adapter field’s meaning; a large country catalogue alone is not coverage proof.
- [ ] Recommend the permitted source combination and explain each retained or rejected candidate. Identify a feasible compatible AI runtime for later selection, or explicitly record the unresolved access/suitability prerequisite; no source/model permission or purchase is inferred from a working key.
