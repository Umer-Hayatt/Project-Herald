## What to build

**Recovery slice:** R09 · phase R4.

Visitors can inspect a real labelled sample and PDF before signing in; readers can complete a clear, accessible finite paper without repeated marketing, unsupported claims or placeholder interfaces.

**Demo:** Open the sample and PDF in a fresh session, sign in and complete a phone/desktop reading session with full Story actions, discovery and a clear finish.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Provide a prominent public sample Edition and matching PDF from permitted validated material with actual date/sample status and no private reader data; samples use the established canonical content path.
- [ ] Simplify signed-in reading to a compact dated header with actual duration versus budget, complete Stories, supported discovery and an ending with archive/PDF actions; remove repetitive promotional/testimonial panels and unnecessary duplicate teasers.
- [ ] Remove unsupported premium-reader claims, unmeasured Trending/editorial labels, decorative controls and reserved-art production copy; verify genuine endorsements before use and label illustrative images honestly.
- [ ] Preserve the established typography/palette and full Story capabilities; any person artwork is human-authored and appropriately permitted, with custom art/motion remaining optional finishing work.
- [ ] Cover mobile/desktop, keyboard, touch, focus, contrast and reduced motion across sign-in, onboarding, reading, settings, archive and PDF actions; give plain-language progress, empty, failed-save and retry states without losing choices.
- [ ] Replay the complete core journey and canonical parity after the final substantive layout change, then batch visual verification and one confirmation; evidence identifies the actual candidate commit.
