# Personal Morning Paper

A personalized daily paper that combines stories relevant to a reader with opportunities to discover important news beyond their usual interests.

## Language

**Beyond Your Bubble**:
A small section of the reader's daily paper containing one or two important stories outside their usual interests, each with an explanation of why it deserves attention.
_Avoid_: Unrelated stories, random recommendations

**Briefing**:
A concise, self-contained explanation of what happened in a story and why it matters, with attribution and links to original reporting.
_Avoid_: Full article, copied article

**Edition**:
A reader's daily paper, created on their first visit of the day and preserved for reading on the website, downloading as a matching PDF, and revisiting in their archive.
_Avoid_: Feed, individual story

**Reading length**:
The reader's preferred time budget for an edition: five, ten, or fifteen minutes.
_Avoid_: Article length

**More like this**:
Explicit feedback that a reader wants greater representation of a story's subject in future personalized selections.
_Avoid_: Click, skip

**Less like this**:
Explicit feedback that a reader wants less representation of a story's subject in future personalized selections.
_Avoid_: Skip, unread

**Skip**:
An action that moves past a story without explicitly expressing dislike of its subject.
_Avoid_: Less like this, dislike

**Story**:
A distinct news event or development represented once in an edition, potentially supported by several source articles.
_Avoid_: Source article, duplicate headline

**Source article**:
An original publisher's report linked as supporting material for a story.
_Avoid_: Briefing, edition

**Preferred region**:
The geographic area whose relevant stories receive extra priority in the reader's paper while important global coverage remains eligible.
_Avoid_: Geographic restriction
